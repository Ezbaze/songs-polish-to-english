# We, the First Brigade

The Legions – a soldier's song,  
The Legions – a sacrificial pyre,  
The Legions – the brazen pride of knights,  
The Legions – the fate of those who risk it all!

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!

Oh, how much anguish, how much suffering,  
Oh, how much blood, tears shed!  
Yet through it all, we did not waver;  
The journey's end gave us strength.

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!

They shouted that we'd been fooled,  
Not believing us – where there's a will, there's a way.  
We shed our blood, standing alone,  
Yet our beloved Commander stood beside us.

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!

Today they see things differently,  
And want to reach our souls,  
And say they now respect us,  
But the time for retribution has come!

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!

We no longer want your recognition,  
Nor your speeches, nor your tears!  
The time for knocking at your hearts  
And at your purses is over!

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!

Now the time has come to settle accounts  
For hearts in anguish and days of torment;  
So do not expect pity;  
The rule is: for blood – demand blood!

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!

Today we – strong in unity –  
Are building Poland – our ancestors' vision.  
In this work, you are not diligent enough;  
Future generations will remember you with shame!

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!

We knew how to fan the sparks  
Of youthful convictions into a fire of zeal,  
To offer our lives for an ideal,  
Our blood, and the dreams we cherished.

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!

Even today, for posterity,  
We can sacrifice the last of our days,  
And sow seeds of nobility amid falsehoods  
With our bodies crushed to pulp, with the fire of our blood.

We, the First Brigade,  
A band of riflemen,  
Have cast our lives and fate upon the pyre,  
Upon the pyre, upon the pyre!
